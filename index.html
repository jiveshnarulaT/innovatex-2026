♥ House Rules & Guidelines

Participant Briefing Checklist

01

Gear & Hardware Requirements

Bring your own laptop, charger, and valid college physical ID card. Campus Wi-Fi is provided, but carrying a personal mobile hotspot is recommended for high bandwidth tasks.

02

Secret Constraints

Constraints for Rewind, Constraint Jam, and Break My Code are revealed strictly at start time. Nothing to prepare in advance—only your core problem-solving capability.

03

Multi-Event Entry

Participants can enter up to two events provided schedule timings do not overlap. Note that Zero Budget Startup runs continuously across all 3 days.

04

Submission Defense

All submissions must be original work. You may use public documentation and developer tools, but you must be able to explain every line of code to the judges upon request.

05

Zero-Spend Integrity

For Zero Budget Startup, spending even 1 Rupee on ads, domains, or premium services results in immediate disqualification. Free tiers and organic hustle only.

06

Responsible Disclosure

For Break My Code, attacks must strictly be confined to assigned opponent applications. Attacks against event infrastructure or non-participating servers are illegal.
